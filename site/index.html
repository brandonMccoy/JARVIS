Local-first voice assistant

J.A.R.V.I.S.

A personal voice assistant that runs entirely on your own computer. It reads your calendar and mail so it can answer questions out loud — and it never asks you to hand your account over to somebody else's server.

What it does

calendar_today

Reads today's events so you can ask what's coming up without opening anything.

calendar_search

Finds events by keyword across your calendars. Read-only — it never creates or edits.

mail_unread

Reports who wrote and roughly what about, from message headers only.

mail_search

Searches your mail with ordinary Gmail queries and reads back the matches.

How it is built

J.A.R.V.I.S. runs as a process on your machine, bound to 127.0.0.1. When you connect a Google account it uses the loopback flow described in RFC 8252: your real system browser opens the genuine Google consent screen, and the authorization code returns to a one-shot listener on a random local port that closes the moment it is done.

  • You supply your own Google OAuth client, so consent runs against your project, not anyone else's.
  • Access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest in a local database and never reach the browser interface.
  • Every scope requested is read-only. Mail bodies are never fetched.
  • There is no J.A.R.V.I.S. server. Nothing about your account is stored anywhere but your machine.
