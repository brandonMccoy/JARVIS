Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026

J.A.R.V.I.S. is software you run on your own computer. There is no J.A.R.V.I.S. server, no account to create, and no operator who can see your data. The developer of this software never receives your calendar, your mail, or your Google credentials.

Who this covers

This policy describes J.A.R.V.I.S., an open-source personal voice assistant installed and run by an individual on their own machine. It is maintained by a single developer, reachable at allontheone@gmail.com.

Because you supply your own Google OAuth client credentials, the consent screen you see runs against your Google Cloud project. Your data never passes through infrastructure belonging to the developer.

What it accesses

J.A.R.V.I.S. requests three read-only scopes. It can never send mail, create events, or change anything in your account.

ScopeWhat it reads
userinfo.email Your Google account email address, shown in the interface so you can tell which account is connected.
calendar.readonly Event titles, times, and locations from your calendars, so it can answer questions about your schedule.
gmail.readonly Message headers only — sender, subject, and date — plus the short preview snippet Gmail itself generates. Message bodies and attachments are never requested.

Mail is fetched with Gmail's format=metadata parameter, which means full message content is never delivered to the application at all.

Where your data goes

Two places, and no others.

  • Your own machine. The application process is bound to 127.0.0.1 and is not reachable from your network. OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest in a local database file. The browser interface is shown connection status only — provider, account email, granted scopes, expiry — and never receives a token.
  • Anthropic's API. To turn a question into a spoken answer, the assistant sends your request and the relevant results — the calendar events or the mail senders, subjects, dates, and snippets it just retrieved — to the Anthropic API, which provides the language model. This happens only when you ask a question that needs them. Anthropic's handling of that data is governed by its own privacy policy.

There is no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, and no third party beyond the two above. Nothing is sold, rented, or shared.

Limited Use disclosure

J.A.R.V.I.S.'s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used solely to provide and improve the assistant's user-facing features, is never used for advertising, and is never sold or transferred except as described above.

Retention and deletion

Calendar and mail data is fetched on demand to answer a question and is not written to a database. Only your OAuth tokens, the connected account email, and the granted scopes are stored, encrypted, on your machine.

Choosing Disconnect in the application revokes the refresh token with Google and erases the stored tokens and account details immediately. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Deleting the application's data directory removes everything it has ever kept.

Security

Tokens are encrypted at rest using a key file stored in the same local data directory. In plain terms: this protects against casual disclosure — a backup, a synced folder, someone glancing at the database — but it does not protect against code already running as you on your own machine. Anyone with access to your user account has access to whatever that account can reach.

Children

J.A.R.V.I.S. is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended for their use.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with an updated date. Material changes to what is accessed or where it is sent will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about what the software does with your data, can go to allontheone@gmail.com.
